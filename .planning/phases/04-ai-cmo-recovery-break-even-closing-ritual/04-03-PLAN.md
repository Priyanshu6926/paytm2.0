     - Log consolidated `CMO_GROWTH` event in `business_events` (`commit=False`) summarizing count of onboarded entries, new customers created, and total credit amount.
     - Execute `conn.commit()`; on any error execute `conn.rollback()` before raising HTTPException.
   - Return `status="CONFIRMED"`, `batch_ref`, `entries_recorded`, `new_customers_created`, `total_onboarded_amount`, `customer_results`, `business_event_id`.
